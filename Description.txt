Use your mods and asset packs in the places the game turns them off: campaign levels, build challenges, and the custom campaign editor.

[h1]Description:[/h1]
Normally the game switches every mod off in build challenges and custom campaigns, and asset packs won't load in the built-in campaign levels. This turns all of that back on. Modded stuff also skips scenario research.

[h1]How to Use:[/h1]
- Turn it on and load one of those parks.
- Each part has its own checkbox in the mod settings:
  - Allow mods in Build Challenges
  - Allow mods in the custom campaign editor and map
  - Load asset packs in campaign levels
  - Modded objects skip scenario research
- The first three apply the next time a park loads.

[h1]Features:[/h1]
- Modded objects start unlocked in scenarios with research and stay out of the research list. The game's own stuff still needs researching.
- Campaign progress and achievements still work like normal.

[h1]Compatibility:[/h1]
- Multiplayer: works, and not everyone needs it.
